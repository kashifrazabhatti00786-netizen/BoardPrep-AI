Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential information to provide personal educational coaching. This includes signup information (email, password hashes via Firebase Authentication), profile variables (such as target Board, Grade Level), and user-uploaded text/image payloads submitted to the Board Answer Checker.

2. How We Use Information

The collected answer excerpts and topics are analyzed using Google's Gemini API endpoints to extract educational feedback. We do not sell, trade, or distribute your personalized performance markers or contact records to external vendors.

3. Data Security and Retention

We leverage industry-leading Firebase security paradigms to encrypt user data in transit and at rest. Your profile records persist strictly while your account remains active. You can request comprehensive data erasure via our support desk.

Help Center

Find answers to common questions regarding BoardPrep AI operations, subscription models, and answer patterns.

How does BoardPrep analyze my handwritten board answers?

You can type your answer directly into the checker, or upload a clear photo/scan of your handwritten response in the Board Answer Checker tab. Our AI engine processes the text and layout to evaluate it against the strict Punjab Board standard guidelines.

Is BoardPrep affiliated with the Punjab Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE)?

No. BoardPrep AI is an independent, state-of-the-art educational auxiliary system. All scoring rules and suggestions are synthesized from historical high-scoring examiner keys and topper layouts, but are not officially endorsed by BISE.

Can I cancel my Topper Plan subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your Profile settings page inside the Dashboard. Standard access remains active until the end of your billing cycle.

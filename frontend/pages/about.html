About BoardPrep AI 2.0

BoardPrep AI 2.0 represents an ambitious, enterprise-grade AI coaching initiative designed to alleviate board exam preparation anxieties for Pakistani Matric and Intermediate students.

Our key architectural goals prioritize curriculum-aligned accuracy. By blending localized Punjab board past paper grading keys with bleeding-edge Large Language Models, BoardPrep acts as a round-the-clock tutor who analyzes writing layouts, structures headings, and recommends appropriate diagrammatic illustrations.

Our development team is comprised of elite software engineers and educational researchers dedicated to democratizing advanced exam preparation tools nationwide.
